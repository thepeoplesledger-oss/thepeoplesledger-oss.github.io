Welcome to **Projects**, where *The People's Ledger* tracks major government initiatives and public commitments in Ghana over time, from announcement through implementation, delay, or abandonment.  
Projects exists because the follow‑up is where accountability actually happens, long after an initiative has faded from the news cycle.
